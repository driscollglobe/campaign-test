Where to find your kWh

On a paper Eversource bill, look for the usage section that lists kWh used for the billing period. It appears near the meter readings.

Every Eversource paper bill also prints a bar chart of your last 13 months of use. A glance at it tells you whether the bill in your hand is a high month, a low month, or a typical one, which matters if you use the single-month option here.

Online, sign in at eversource.com and open the usage view. It lists each of the last 12 months of use, and the 12-month total or average is exactly what this tool asks for.
