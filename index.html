What could Eversource's rate request add to your bill?

Eversource has asked Connecticut regulators for higher electric delivery rates. Enter your usage and this tool estimates what the request, as filed, could add for a home on standard residential Rate 1.

First, one quick question

Does your home have solar panels or another net metering arrangement? Solar billing works differently, so we check before showing a number.

Where to find your kWh

On a paper Eversource bill, look for the usage section that lists kWh used for the billing period. It appears near the meter readings.

Online, sign in at eversource.com and open your usage history. It shows each of the last 12 months, and the 12-month total or average is exactly what this tool asks for.

We can't give your home a reliable number

Solar billing arrangements vary and this calculator may not accurately estimate your increase.
